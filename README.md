Complete ecommerce platform for MUHAJ Multi Biz.

## Planned system

### Customer
- Homepage
- Product browsing
- Categories
- Search
- Product details
- Product gallery
- Wishlist
- Cart
- Checkout
- Payments
- Orders
- Order tracking
- Customer account
- Reviews

### Administration
- Dashboard
- Product management
- Product image management
- Category management
- Inventory
- Order management
- Customer management
- Payment management
- Reviews
- Coupons and promotions
- Reports
- Store settings

## Technology

- Next.js
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Supabase
- PostgreSQL
- Supabase Authentication
- Supabase Storage
- Paystack integration

## Development principle

Every major feature must pass:

- ESLint
- TypeScript
- Production build

before the next major feature is added.
